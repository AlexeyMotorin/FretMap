## Description

FretMap is a visual music companion for guitarists. It brings together an interactive fretboard, chord shapes, scales, modes, and practical harmony tools in one focused app.

Explore the fretboard:
• choose a key and scale or mode;
• display note names and scale degrees;
• highlight scale degrees with colors;
• switch between sharps and flats;
• select the number of strings and instrument tuning;
• create, save, edit, and delete custom tunings.

Work with chords:
• find essential chord shapes on the fretboard;
• browse multiple shapes for the same chord;
• explore triads and seventh chords;
• use 6-, 7-, and 8-string guitars;
• adapt chord shapes to the selected tuning.

Understand harmony:
• explore functional and modal harmony;
• build your own chord progressions;
• choose triads or seventh chords for each scale degree;
• discover popular progressions;
• save useful progressions to Favorites;
• create a personal progression library;
• hear progressions played with piano sounds;
• adjust the playback tempo.

FretMap helps you find notes faster, understand how chords are built, and turn music theory into practical knowledge on your instrument.

The app works offline, requires no account, and stores your settings and saved content locally on your device.
